## ✨ Core Highlights

### 🔴 Complete Redstone System
- ✅ Full component support: redstone wire, redstone torch, repeater, comparator, redstone lamp, door, trapdoor, hopper, dispenser, dropper, note block, etc.
- ⚡ High-frequency redstone pulses and same-tick update handling, eliminating lag and delay anomalies
- 🧭 Repeaters/comparators are placed precisely according to the player's viewpoint, with direction logic 100% faithful to vanilla

### 🌾 Realistic Farmland Mechanics
- 👣 Trampling degradation: farmland degradation is triggered when players/entities fall from a height
- 💧 Dry-out from lack of water: farmland gradually loses water and, in severe cases, reverts to dirt
- 🌱 Crop protection: when degradation occurs, crops above are automatically popped off, with behavior exactly matching vanilla

### 🌍 Immersive Terrain Generation
- 🌿 Surface vegetation and biome structures are richer and more natural
- 🌻 Support for two-block-high plants (tall grass, double-height plants, etc.)
- 📐 Vegetation distribution is orderly and regular, no longer sparse and chaotic
